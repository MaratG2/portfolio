## Hiring targets

Prioritize ML Engineer vacancies around agents, automation, AI platforms and production systems. Evaluate the actual responsibilities and seniority, not the title alone. For international positions, check location eligibility and working-hour requirements for each employer.

Keep the software engineering background visible. Do not claim model-research experience or an unsupported level of seniority.

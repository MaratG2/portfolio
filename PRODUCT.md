# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Hiring managers and recruiters considering Marat Gazizulin for ML Engineer roles in Russia and internationally.

## Product Purpose
Present a clear, credible professional profile and make résumé download and contact straightforward.

## Positioning
ML Engineer focused on AI agents, business process automation and end-to-end systems engineering.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Bilingual Russian and English. Shared content for the site and PDFs. Current-employer details are restricted to the approved experience sentence in docs/career/evidence-register.md. No current-employer project descriptions or metrics. No location or current-job strip in the hero.

## Brand Commitments
Use the user's existing portrait and name. Russian specialization is localized. Preserve historical software engineering experience. User explicitly authorizes a full visual redesign and animation.

## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied career history, education and portrait. No public customer testimonials or open-source adoption claims.

## Product Principles
Clear role positioning. Respect confidentiality. Keep content readable without JavaScript. Make contact and CV access immediate.

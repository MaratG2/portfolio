# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Hiring managers and recruiters considering Marat Gazizulin for ML Engineer roles in Russia and internationally.

## Product Purpose
Present a clear, credible professional profile and make résumé download and contact straightforward.

## Positioning
ML Engineer focused on AI systems, agents, RAG and business process automation. Lead with system design, technical ownership, automated evaluation and delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Bilingual Russian and English. Shared content for the site and PDFs. Current-employer details are restricted to the approved experience sentence in docs/career/evidence-register.md. No current-employer project descriptions or metrics. No location or current-job strip in the hero.

## Brand Commitments
Use the user's existing portrait and name. Russian specialization is localized. Preserve historical software engineering experience. The October 2026 redesign replaces the green-and-white panels with the dark "Signal to System" direction in DESIGN.md; mint green remains the single accent. The portrait appears as the hero particle film and must stay the user's own photo.

## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied career history, education and portrait. Artillect: multimodal platform architecture, company GPU models, external APIs, task distribution and prompt-generation RAG. Adventarium: multi-stage workflows, automated dataset evaluation, tracing and monitoring. General Langfuse experience. No invented evaluation scores or adoption claims.

## Product Principles
Finished recruiter-facing content only; no placeholders, future-project claims or editorial assessment notes. Clear role positioning. Respect confidentiality. Keep content readable without JavaScript. Make contact and CV access immediate.
